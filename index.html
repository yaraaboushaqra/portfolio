Aurelia shapes the visual world around watches: launches, editorial portfolios, boutique stories, collector campaigns, and still-life systems.

Her work combines disciplined grids, restrained colour, product hierarchy, macro texture, and a sharp editorial voice — allowing the craft of each timepiece to lead the image.
